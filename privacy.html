Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

The app accesses Google Drive only for the owner's own account, using Google's https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive scope, so rclone can list, upload, download, and update the owner's files.

Data stays between Google Drive and the owner's own computer. The app runs no servers of its own, and it doesn't collect, store, sell, or share any user data with third parties.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: questions about this policy can be raised with the site owner through the GitHub profile github.com/wilbergerhausen-spec.
